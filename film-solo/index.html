The solo cut

The other half, and the quiet opposite of the Arena film. This one leads on what makes Tadapop different from every other habit app: there is nothing to sign up for, nothing watching you, and it works with the aeroplane mode on.

Watch it

A checklist for your day — and nothing else.

Six scenes, voiced, captioned, about 49 seconds. Sound starts when you press play.

What it covers

  1. No account, no feed, works on a plane.
  2. Put the things you actually meant to do on it.
  3. Tap one done, count the water or the pages, or start a timer and disappear.
  4. Clear the whole day and it pays out one Tpoint. Miss one and it does not.
  5. The streak goes up. Break it and nothing scolds you.
  6. A year later the grid has gone green — and nobody had to see it but you.
