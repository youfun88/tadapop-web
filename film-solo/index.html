What do people ask about daily missions?

Do I need an account to use the daily checklist?

No. Your own missions work fully offline and need no account. You need an account only for the Check-in Club.

How can I log a mission?

Three ways: tap to check it off, count toward a target, or run a countdown timer that chimes at zero.

How do I earn a Tpoint?

Clear every mission due that day and you earn 1 Tpoint. Clear only some of them and you earn 0.

What happens to my streak if I miss a day?

A streak freeze protects one missed day automatically. You earn one every 14 active days and can hold up to 3. Freezes cannot be bought.

How many missions can I keep for free?

5 active missions at a time. Archiving one frees its slot, and Tadapop Pro removes the limit.
