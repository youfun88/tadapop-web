Chore chart app or a routine checklist your child ticks off?

Many chore chart apps are built around the parent: you assign chores from your own phone, approve them, and some pay allowance. Tadapop works differently. The routine lives on your child’s own tablet, they tick each mission off themselves, and nothing involves money.

Tadapop is a habit tracker for anyone. Solo tracking needs no account, so children of any age can use it, and without an account nothing is sent to us. The step-by-step setup is on the kids’ daily routine checklist page.

01 · THE DIFFERENCE

How is Tadapop different from a chore chart app?

  • Where it runs: on your child’s own iPad, iPhone or Android tablet, with no sign-in. There is no family account to set up.
  • Who ticks it off: your child taps each mission when it is done. You don’t approve each one.
  • Where you see progress: on the Stats screen of your child’s device: the streak, the week, per-mission completion rates and a full-year heatmap. There is no remote parent view.
  • Money: none. Tadapop does not pay allowance or handle money, and there are no prizes that convert to money. A day where every mission due is done earns one Tpoint inside the app.
  • Cost: solo tracking is free with up to five active missions at a time; Tadapop Pro removes that limit.

02 · WHICH FITS

When is a chore chart app the better choice?

  • You want to pay allowance or pocket money for chores through the app.
  • You want to assign chores and check them off from your own phone.
  • You want several children and parents on one shared family account.

Tadapop does none of these. If that is what you need, a chore chart app is the better fit.

03 · WHEN TADAPOP FITS

When does a routine checklist on the child’s tablet fit?

  • Your child does the same steps each day, like a morning, after-school or bedtime routine, and you want them to tick it off themselves.
  • You would rather keep the data on the device: without an account, missions, logs, notes and photos stay there.
  • You want some tasks only on school days: each mission can be due every day, on chosen weekdays, a few times a week, or once.

What do parents ask when choosing between them?

Is Tadapop a chore chart app?

Not quite. It is a habit tracker your child uses on their own tablet: each step of a routine is a mission they tick off. There is no parent account, no approval step and no allowance.

Can I pay allowance with Tadapop?

No. Tadapop does not handle money, and there are no prizes that convert to money. If you agree a reward with your child, that stays between you.

Can I assign tasks from my own phone?

No. Without an account the data stays on your child’s device, so you set up missions and look at the Stats screen on their tablet.

Does my child need an account?

No. Solo tracking needs no account. An account is needed only for the Check-in Club, and accounts are for ages 13 and up.

Is it free?

Solo tracking is free. The free plan keeps five active missions at a time; Tadapop Pro removes that limit.
