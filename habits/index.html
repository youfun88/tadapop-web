What do people ask about building a habit?

How do you build a habit that sticks?

Start so small you can’t say no. Tie the new habit to something you already do. Plan a backup for the hard day before it comes. Then give it time. The best habit books agree on these points, and the film above covers them in about two minutes.

How long does it take to form a habit?

It varies a lot. One study (Lally and colleagues, 2009) found a median of about 66 days for a new habit to feel automatic, ranging from 18 to 254 days across people. That is one study’s finding, not a rule.

What happens if I miss a day?

One missed day changes almost nothing, so be kind to yourself and pick it up again. In a Tadapop habit program, a missed day never resets anything: the program counts the days you’ve done.

What is a Tadapop habit program?

A guided 4-week plan for one new habit. Pick an area and a goal, or name your own habit, then answer a few short questions. The program adds one tiny habit to Today, tied to a moment you already have. It grows each week after a one-tap check-in and keeps a smaller backup ready for hard days.

Are Tadapop’s habit programs free?

Yes, for now. Programs are free while they’re new, and one you start now stays free until you finish it. A program doesn’t count toward the free plan’s limit of five missions.
