New to Tadapop? Three steps

  1. Install the app from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Come back to this page and tap “Open in Tadapop”. Installing doesn't carry the invite across on its own — this link is what does it.
  3. Sign in with your email. The Arena needs an account so your friends can see your name. Tadapop emails you a 6-digit code — there's no password. Your own missions never need an account at all.

If the button doesn't open the app, launch Tadapop yourself, go to Arena → Join code and type the code above.
