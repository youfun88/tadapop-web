Want a good habit, but the motivation fades in a few days?

You mean to work out, read every day, get to bed earlier or spend less time on your phone, and a few days in, it slips away.

Tadapop brings daily missions, habit building and check-in challenges with friends together in one app.

Set your own goals and track them each day, or invite friends to take one on with you. Remind each other, share how it went, and make a good habit easier to keep.

01 · DAILY MISSIONS

Daily missions: a little every day

Turn what you want to do into small missions you can finish each day.

  • Check off: a workout, reading, an early night. Done is one tap.
  • Count: 8 glasses of water, 8,000 steps. Build toward the target.
  • Timer: read for 30 minutes, focus for 25, with a countdown.
  • Flexible schedules: every day, chosen weekdays, a few times a week, or once.

Open Tadapop and see what’s on for today. Finish one, tick one off. Simple, and it feels good.

02 · HABIT PROGRAMS

Habit programs: not sure where to start? Let Tadapop help

Want to build a new habit but don’t know the first step?

Pick an area of life you want to improve, like fitness, sleep, food, study, money or screen time. Answer a few simple questions and Tadapop lays out a 4-week program that builds one step at a time.

  • Start with the smallest habit you can actually do.
  • Attach the new habit to something already in your routine.
  • Adjust the goal each week to how it’s really going.
  • Busy days get an easier fallback.
  • Miss a day now and then without starting over.

You don’t have to be perfect on day one. What matters is building a habit you can keep. Watch the 2-minute film →

03 · CHECK-IN CLUB

Private Check-in Club: take it on with friends

Easy to give up on your own? Bring your friends!

A 30-day workout challenge, reading every day, an early-to-bed plan, or any goal you want to reach together?

Start your own check-in club in Tadapop, invite friends, set the rules together, check in and follow each other’s progress.

  • Host your own: set the challenge name, goal, frequency and check-in rules.
  • Invite-only: only the friends you invite can join. No public community, no strangers.
  • Photo and video check-ins: share proof it’s done, so the effort shows.
  • Live leaderboard: see where everyone stands.
  • Group chat: share how it’s going, cheer each other on, and reply to friends’ check-ins.
  • Bring a personal mission along: turn it into a challenge with friends, without touching your own record.

What do people ask about Tadapop?

What app lets friends do a habit challenge with a leaderboard?

Tadapop’s Check-in Club does. A host creates a private challenge and invites friends. Everyone checks in once a day, and a live leaderboard counts how many days each person hit the target. There is no public feed and there are no strangers.

Is there an app where whoever skips their workout buys dinner?

You can run that deal in Tadapop. Agree it among yourselves, for example four runs a week and whoever skips buys dinner, then set it up as a challenge so the leaderboard shows who turned up. The dinner stays between you and your friends: no challenge in the app involves real money, prizes or stakes.

Can I use Tadapop on my own, without an account?

Yes. Personal missions work offline and need no account. You need an account only for the Check-in Club.

Is Tadapop free?

Yes, it is free to start and has no ads. The free plan runs up to 5 personal missions at a time and hosts 1 challenge, and joining a friend’s challenge is never limited. Tadapop Pro removes the limits on missions and on challenges you host.

Where can I get Tadapop?

On the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and on Google Play for Android.
