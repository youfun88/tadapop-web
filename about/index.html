What Tadapop does

Tadapop keeps your daily habits on one quiet screen, and lets you check in on a goal together with friends. Two parts, one app.

PART 1 · TODAY

Today’s missions: your own habits

Today shows only what’s due today. Tap it done and get on with your day.

  • Check off: done or not, one tap.
  • Count: toward a target, like 8 glasses or 8,000 steps.
  • Timer: a countdown that chimes at zero.

Set each one for every day, chosen weekdays, a few times a week, or a single date.

Habit programs: start a new habit the easy way

Want a new habit but not sure where to begin? Pick an area (sleep, fitness, food, stress, screen time, money, study, faith and more) and answer a few short questions. Tadapop builds a 4-week plan and puts one tiny habit on Today, tied to something you already do:

“After brushing my teeth → straight to bed, no scrolling.”

  • Week 1 is the smallest step that still counts.
  • A smaller backup is ready for hard days.
  • A one-tap weekly check-in: grow it, keep it, or make it smaller.
  • Miss a day and nothing resets.

Habit programs are free while they’re new. Watch the 2-minute film →

PART 2 · CLUB

Check-in Club: challenges with friends

Pick a goal with people you actually know, like a walk every day for 30 days or reading four nights a week, and check in each day.

  • Invite-only. No strangers, no public lists, no real money.
  • Proof and notes. If the host asks for proof, add up to three photos or videos to your check-in. Friends can reply right under it. The host writes the rules in a note everyone reads before they join.
  • A live leaderboard and a group chat for the running commentary.
  • Any habit can become a challenge with Challenge Friends. Your own habit stays as it is.

How a challenge is scored: Club Rules →

Free, and quiet

  • Tracking your own habits is free, works offline, and needs no account.
  • Sign in only for the Check-in Club, or a backup across phones.
  • Reminders are local. The only push is a message from your challenge, and you can mute one or all of them.
  • No ads, and no shaming when you miss a day.
