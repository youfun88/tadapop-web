Want a good habit, but the motivation fades in a few days?

You mean to work out, read every day, get to bed earlier or spend less time on your phone, and a few days in, it slips away.

Tadapop brings daily missions, habit building and check-in challenges with friends together in one app. Set your own goals and track them each day, or invite friends to take one on with you. Remind each other, share how it went, and make a good habit easier to keep.

01 · DAILY MISSIONS

Daily missions: a little every day

Turn what you want to do into small missions you can finish each day.

  • Check off: a workout, reading, an early night. Done is one tap.
  • Count: 8 glasses of water, 8,000 steps. Build toward the target.
  • Timer: read for 30 minutes, focus for 25, with a countdown.
  • Flexible schedules: every day, chosen weekdays, a few times a week, or once.

Open Tadapop and see what’s on for today. Finish one, tick one off. Simple, and it feels good.

02 · HABIT PROGRAMS

Habit programs: not sure where to start? Let Tadapop help

Want to build a new habit but don’t know the first step? Pick an area of life you want to improve, like fitness, sleep, food, study, money or screen time. Answer a few simple questions and Tadapop lays out a 4-week program that builds one step at a time.

  • Start with the smallest habit you can actually do.
  • Attach the new habit to something already in your routine.
  • Adjust the goal each week to how it’s really going.
  • Busy days get an easier fallback.
  • Miss a day now and then without starting over.

You don’t have to be perfect on day one. What matters is building a habit you can keep. Watch the 2-minute film →

03 · CHECK-IN CLUB

Private Check-in Club: take it on with friends

Easy to give up on your own? Bring your friends. A 30-day workout challenge, reading every day, an early-to-bed plan, or any goal you want to reach together. Start your own check-in club in Tadapop, invite friends, set the rules together, check in and follow each other’s progress.

  • Host your own: set the challenge name, goal, frequency and check-in rules.
  • Invite-only: only the friends you invite can join. No public community, no strangers.
  • Photo and video check-ins: share proof it’s done, so the effort shows.
  • Live leaderboard: see where everyone stands.
  • Group chat: share how it’s going, cheer each other on, and reply to friends’ check-ins.
  • Bring a personal mission along: turn it into a challenge with friends, without touching your own record.

Friends, family, classmates or coworkers: anyone can start a check-in club of their own. Club Rules →
