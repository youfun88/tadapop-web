What's new in this build

A big update over the June build — the whole app has been restyled, and the Arena has grown up.

Arena

  • Group chat in every challenge — text, photos, video and voice messages.
  • A proof feed showing what everyone logged, with stills for video proof.
  • Arena Score and a podium, so standings read at a glance.
  • Real cadence — a daily reset hour, a grace period, a day grid, at-risk warnings, and backfilling a missed day.
  • Custom challenge lengths and a much simpler convene flow.
  • Invite links that open the app with the code already filled in.

Solo tracking

  • Drag to reorder today's missions — long-press to start.
  • One-tap undo on a mission you completed by mistake.
  • Rewards and progression — cosmetics and trophies as you level up.
  • Your data follows your account — sign in and everything you tracked locally comes with you.

How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android above and open the downloaded .apk file.
  2. If Android warns about an unknown source, tap Settings → enable Allow from this source, then go back.
  3. Tap Install, then open Tadapop from your app drawer.

Already have the June build? This installs straight over it — no need to uninstall first, and your missions stay put.

Good to know

  • Android only. This is an .apk — it can't be installed on an iPhone.
  • Solo tracking needs no account. An account (a quick email code) is only needed for the Arena.
  • Opening an invite link? On Android it lands on a web page first — tap Open in Tadapop there to jump into the app.
  • This is a pre-release test build, so expect rough edges — that's what we're testing.
