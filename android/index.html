Install Tadapop on Android

v1.4.5 · on Google Play since 29 August 2026

Tadapop is on Google Play. That is the way in for almost everyone: it installs like any other app, updates itself, and skips the unknown-source warning entirely.

If you can't use Google Play

Some devices ship without the Play Store. For those, the same v1.4.5 build is available as a direct .apk. Everything works the same — but nothing updates itself, so you have to come back here for each new version. If Play is an option, use Play.

What's new in this build

The rewards system has been rebuilt, and cosmetics finally do something.

Rewards

  • Nine trophies instead of five — the first arrives on your very first clean day, with more at 7 and 14 days.
  • Live progress everywhere. A locked trophy now reads “12 / 30” instead of only telling you what it wants.
  • Tap anything locked to see exactly what earns it and how far off you are.
  • Info icons on every Profile section, explaining how that track actually works.

Cosmetics you can wear

  • Accent channels and panel themes now apply. Tap an earned one to wear it, tap again to take it off.
  • Your look follows your account — sign in and it comes with you to a new phone.
  • Earned app icons are iPhone-only. Changing the icon on Android means closing and reopening the app and can break home-screen shortcuts, so Android keeps one icon.

How to install the APK

  1. Tap Download the APK instead above and open the downloaded .apk file.
  2. If Android warns about an unknown source, tap Settings → enable Allow from this source, then go back.
  3. Tap Install, then open Tadapop from your app drawer.

Already have the July build? This installs straight over it — no need to uninstall first, and your missions stay put.

Good to know

  • Android only. This is an .apk — it can't be installed on an iPhone.
  • Solo tracking needs no account. An account (a quick email code) is only needed for the Arena.
  • Opening an invite link? On Android it lands on a web page first — tap Open in Tadapop there to jump into the app.
  • Installed the APK before? Play won't recognise it as the same app — uninstall the sideloaded copy first if you want Play to handle updates from now on. Signed-in Arena data comes back when you sign in; solo data on that device does not.
