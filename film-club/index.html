The Club cut

One film, one half of the app. A challenge from the moment somebody starts it to the moment somebody buys dinner — and nothing else, so it never has to stop and explain that your own habits are a different screen.

Watch it

Nobody sticks to it alone.

Eight scenes, voiced, captioned, about 61 seconds. Sound starts when you press play.

What it covers

  1. Pick one thing, then pick the people who will notice.
  2. Invite-only. No strangers, no feed.
  3. The host writes the deal in their own words — the forfeit, and what it pays for.
  4. The daily check-in: do the work, then come back and log it.
  5. Proof somebody else can see — real check-ins, not stock photos.
  6. The board does the nagging, so nobody has to.
  7. Fifty days later, somebody is buying dinner. And all of you trained.
