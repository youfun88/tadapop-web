One point a day, at most

A day counts once every mission due that day is done. Half a day counts for nothing, and a heroic day counts for one — the only input is turning up again tomorrow.

Reached, never bought

Nothing here is for sale and nothing is deducted. Your point total simply passes a mark and the thing opens.

Nothing is taken back

Your record only goes up. A badge stays on the shelf for good, a cosmetic you've opened never closes, and a missed day doesn't undo any of it.

The road

Every point where something changes, from your first clean day to a full year. Nothing in between is a dead stretch — the next thing is always in sight. This is the solo road; Champion and Contender aren't on it, because they're won in the Arena and no number of clean days will open them.

Ranks

Your standing, measured in clean days. It only ever climbs — and every rung has its own insignia, so a promotion is something you can see rather than a word that quietly changed. Tap one to watch the moment.

Streak freezes

A streak freeze quietly protects a day you missed, so a bad week doesn't erase a good month. One arrives every 14 days you do at least one thing, up to 3 held at once — showing up is what earns protection, not being perfect. They're earned, never bought, so holding one can never cost you a rank or close a cosmetic you've already opened.
