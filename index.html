THE ACTUAL APP

Not a mockup. This is the build on Google Play.

Today shows only what is actually due — nothing else. Each mission is one tap, one count, or one timer, and the bar at the top tells you how far off the day's Tpoint you are.

SIX RULES

Why this one, and not another habit app.

▲

One Tpoint a day, or none

Clear every mission due and the day pays exactly 1 Tpoint. Clearing some pays nothing, and doing extra earns nothing. A lifetime total is simply a count of clean days.

❄

Streak freezes are earned, never bought

One arrives every 14 active days, you can hold three, and one is spent automatically on a day you miss. A bad day does not cost you a run you spent months building.

◷

Your day starts at 4am

Or whenever you say it does. Finishing something at 1am counts toward the day it belongs to, not the one the calendar insists on.

⌁

Offline, with no account

Solo tracking runs entirely on your device. Sign in only if you want the Arena or a backup across phones — everything else works with no connection and no sign-up.

⊘

No ads, no trackers

No advertising, and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the app. Proof photos and notes stay on your device.

⚔

The Arena is invite-only

Private challenges with people you actually know. No public discovery, no matchmaking, no strangers, and no real money anywhere near it.

THE ARENA

Competition with the people who keep you honest.

Set a goal and a length, decide whether a photo or video is required to check in, and invite the people you want. There is a group thread for the running commentary, and a live leaderboard that counts one thing: how many days you hit the target. Exceeding it banks nothing.

Nothing is staked and nothing converts to money. Losing a challenge costs you nothing at all, and joining someone else's challenge is free on every plan, forever.

Questions people actually ask.

Is Tadapop free?

Yes. Tracking your own habits is free, needs no account, and stays that way. A free account keeps five active missions and hosts one challenge at a time. Pro removes both limits for US$3.99 a month, or less per month on the yearly plan.

What is a Tpoint?

Clear every mission due on a given day and that day pays exactly one Tpoint. Clearing some of them pays nothing, and there is no way to earn more by doing extra. So a lifetime total of 128 Tpoints means 128 days you cleared completely.

Does it work offline?

Completely. Solo tracking runs on your device and needs no connection. Sign in only if you want the Arena, or a backup across phones.

Do I need an account?

Not to track anything. An account is required only for the Arena, because a private challenge has to know who you are to the other players.

Is Android still free?

Free is free on Android, exactly as it is on iPhone: track anything with no account, and a free account keeps five active missions and hosts one challenge at a time. The trial period when Android had no billing at all has ended — Pro is on sale in Google Play at the same US$3.99 a month, and the same two limits apply.

Is it on the App Store and Google Play?

Both of them. Version 1.4.5 is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and the same version is on Google Play for Android — so it installs like any other app on either phone. TestFlight is finished: if you were on the beta, install the App Store version over it and everything you have logged stays where it is.
