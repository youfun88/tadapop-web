Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. Solo habit tracking works fully offline with no account. You only need an account to use the Arena — the private competition feature.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to your profile/settings, and choose delete account. This removes your cloud profile and associated Arena data. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.

How is the Arena scored?

Your position on a challenge leaderboard is how many days you hit the daily target, and finishing near the top can earn Arena Score. The full payout, the tie rules, and the rank ladder are on the Arena Rules page.

Is my data private?

Yes. Solo data stays on your device. Account data is protected so only you (and the challenges you're part of) can access it. We don't sell data or run ad trackers. See our Privacy Policy.

Which platforms are supported?

Tadapop runs on iPhone and Android. On Android, install version 1.4.2 from Google Play. On iPhone the App Store listing is not public yet — 1.4.2 was submitted on 23 August 2026 and is still waiting for review — so until it clears install through TestFlight — the QR codes for both are on our home page. No Play Store on your device? There is a direct APK.
